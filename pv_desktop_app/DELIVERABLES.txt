                    📋 DECISION CALCULATION RULES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

As specified in requirements:

Grade Range  | Decision              | Mention              | Color
─────────────┼───────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼──────
≥ 16         | Admis                 | Très Bien            | 🟢 Green
14 - 16      | Admis                 | Bien                 | 🟢 Green
12 - 14      | Admis                 | Assez Bien           | 🟢 Green
10 - 12      | Admis                 | Passable             | 🟢 Green
8 - 10       | Ajourné               | Session de rattrapage | 🟠 Orange
< 8          | Ajourné / Exclus      | (empty)              | 🔴 Red

Implementation:
  • Fully automatic calculation in Etudiant.cs
  • CalculerDecisionEtMention() method
  • Called during Excel import
  • Displayed in DataGrid with color coding

                    🚀 QUICK START INSTRUCTIONS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PREREQUISITES:
  • Windows 7 or later
  • .NET Framework 4.8
  • MySQL 5.7+ (XAMPP recommended)
  • 100 MB free disk space

STEP 1: Start MySQL
  1. Open XAMPP Control Panel
  2. Click "Start" for MySQL
  3. Verify MySQL shows "ON" ✅

STEP 2: Launch Application
  1. Double-click: F:\pv de deliberation\bin\Debug\DesktopApp.exe
  2. Application opens in 2-3 seconds

STEP 3: Import Excel
  1. Click tab: "📥 Import Excel"
  2. Click button: "Parcourir..." (Browse)
  3. Select your Excel file
  4. Click button: "✅ Charger et valider"
  5. View data in table ✨

STEP 4: Verify Results
  • Check that all columns display correctly
  • Verify Decision and Mention are calculated automatically
  • Look for color coding (green = Admis, orange = Rattrapage, 
    red = Ajourné)

DONE! The application is working. 🎉
